About Our Department

  • Our department is committed to providing high-quality academic education
    that adapts to the latest technological advancements and prepares students for the labor market.

  • We offer state-of-the-art computer laboratories, distinguished faculty members,
    and specialized training programs in software engineering and artificial intelligence.

Academic Departments

General Department

Provides foundational courses in mathematics, logic, basic programming, and core computer science principles.

Software Engineering

Deals with building large-scale applications, system design, web development, and software testing.

Artificial Intelligence

Explores machine learning, neural networks, data analysis, and smart automation systems.

Cybersecurity

Deals with protecting networks, systems, programs, and data from digital attacks, cyber threats, and unauthorized access.
